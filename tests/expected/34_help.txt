REWR term : alg [,alg ..]   rewrites term with the rule sets and prints the result
t = REWR term : alg         stores the result in the TERM t
rule sets: All (simplification), Integrate, RubiIntegrate, Solve (inc/solve), ...
Int(f,x)   the indefinite integral of f by x:
  REWR Int(x^2,x) : Integrate          (a small rule set)
  REWR Int(1/(1+x^2),x) : RubiIntegrate  (the translated Rubi rules)
